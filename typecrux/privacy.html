Privacy Policy — TypeCrux

Publisher: RahaKit

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Version: 1.4

Contact: rahakit.help@gmail.com

Summary

TypeCrux is a local-first text expander for Google Chrome. Everything you

create with it — your snippets, data tables, contacts, settings and usage

statistics — is stored only in your own browser, on your own device

(chrome.storage.local). TypeCrux has no user accounts, no server of ours

ever receives your content, and the extension contains no analytics, no

advertising and no tracking of any kind.

The only network communication the product itself initiates is the

validation of a Pro license key with Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider,

when you activate or re-check a license. This policy explains all of the

above in full, including exactly what we never collect, why the extension

asks for each permission, and the small amount of information that exists

when you buy Pro or write to us for support.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How we make money

We sell TypeCrux Pro subscriptions and lifetime licenses through

Lemon Squeezy. **We do not sell, rent or monetise your data, and we never

will.** There is no advertising in the product and no data business behind

it. If this ever changed, this policy would have to change first — see

"Changes to this policy".

Definitions used in this policy

  • Your Content — the snippets, data tables, contacts and other material

you create or import into TypeCrux.

  • Settings & Stats — your preferences and the usage counters the

extension keeps locally (for example, how many expansions you have made).

These exist only on your device.

  • Personal Data — information that identifies a person. As explained

below, the extension itself gives us none; Personal Data only reaches us

if you buy Pro (through Lemon Squeezy) or email us for support.

What the extension stores, and where

TypeCrux stores Your Content, your Settings & Stats, any license key you

activate, and its own automatic local backups (snapshots) in Chrome's local

extension storage on your device. This storage:

  • never leaves your browser except through an export you initiate

(the extension can save an export file to your Downloads folder, under

your control, when you ask it to);

  • is not synchronised by us to any cloud service;
  • is deleted when you delete the data inside the product or uninstall the

extension.

What we never collect

TypeCrux does not collect, record, transmit or sell:

  • your keystrokes, beyond the trigger-detection described below;
  • the text you type, the text of your snippets, or the contents of web

pages you visit;

  • your browsing history or a list of websites you use;
  • your clipboard contents (the extension reads the clipboard only when you

ask it to — when an expansion you triggered inserts {clipboard}, or when you

click a 📋 Clipboard button to create a snippet from what you copied — and

nothing is stored or sent anywhere);

  • analytics, telemetry, crash reports or usage statistics of any kind —

the statistics shown in the product are computed and kept locally;

The usage counters behind those statistics (expansion count, estimated time saved, active days) and the state of the one-time Chrome Web Store review prompt are likewise stored only on your device, are used only to operate the statistics and that prompt, and are never transmitted anywhere;

  • any advertising identifiers, and it shows no ads.

Trigger detection. To expand your shortcuts, TypeCrux's content

script runs on pages you visit and watches what you type **locally, inside

the page**, solely to recognise when you have typed one of your triggers

followed by your expand key. Nothing you type is stored, logged or

transmitted; a page's content is never collected.

Autofill. Where TypeCrux offers form autofill, it acts only when you

explicitly invoke it, shows you a preview, and fills only after your

confirmation. The processing happens locally.

The only network communication

The extension initiates exactly one kind of network communication:

  • License validation. When you activate a Pro license key (and on

occasional re-checks), the extension contacts Lemon Squeezy's license

API, sending the license key and the activation details Lemon Squeezy

requires (such as an instance identifier and the product/variant the key

belongs to). No snippet content, settings or statistics are ever

included.

Two further interactions are handled by Chrome or by you, not by us:

  • Updates. Chrome checks the Chrome Web Store for extension updates;

that is Chrome's own behaviour, under Google's policies.

  • Links you open. If you open links from inside the product (for

example this policy, the store page or a purchase page), the destination

site's own privacy policy applies to that visit.

Buying Pro — payments and Lemon Squeezy

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as merchant of

record. Your payment card details go to Lemon Squeezy and **never reach

us**. Lemon Squeezy shares with us the order details we need to run the

store and support you — such as the email address used at checkout, the

product purchased and the license key issued. Lemon Squeezy's own privacy

policy governs the data it processes:

https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy

When you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the content

of your message. We use it only to answer you and to keep the conversation

coherent. We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed

for support and ordinary business records, and you may ask us to delete it

at any time (see "Your rights"). This — together with the purchase records

held by Lemon Squeezy — is the only Personal Data about our users that

exists outside your own device.

The product also offers a Copy diagnostics button (Settings → Help

& support). It copies a short report — the extension version, your plan

and counts of your snippets, folders, tables and contacts — so you can

paste it into a support email if you choose. It never includes the text

of your snippets, contact details, anything from your pages or any part

of your license key, and nothing is sent unless you paste it into an

email yourself.

TypeCrux requests no host permissions and cannot read or change website

data outside the page interactions described in this policy.

Cookies

The extension sets no cookies. This website (our GitHub Pages site)

sets no cookies and runs no analytics; GitHub, as host, may process

technical information such as visitor IP addresses in server logs under

GitHub's own privacy statement.

Data security

Your Content never reaches a server of ours, so there is no central store

of user content that could be breached. Your local data is protected by

Chrome's own profile and extension-storage security on your device, and we

recommend keeping your operating system and browser accounts secured.

Data you send us by email is held in our support mailbox, which we protect

with strong authentication, and is used only as described above.

Your rights and choices

Inside the product, you are in direct control. You can view, edit,

export (in open formats) and delete everything TypeCrux stores, at any

time, from Settings → Import / Export and the in-product delete controls.

Uninstalling the extension removes its local storage.

For the little data that exists outside your device (support emails,

and purchase records held by Lemon Squeezy): if you are in the EU/EEA, the

UK, California or another region with privacy rights, you may ask us to

access, correct, export or delete your Personal Data, and to object to or

restrict its processing. Write to rahakit.help@gmail.com and we will

respond within 30 days. We will never discriminate against you for

exercising these rights. For purchase data, Lemon Squeezy's policy and

dashboard govern the records it holds as merchant of record.

Do Not Sell / Do Not Share. We do not sell or share Personal Data for

advertising or for any other purpose. There are no trackers to opt out of.

Children's privacy

TypeCrux is a productivity tool not directed at children, and we do not

knowingly collect Personal Data from children under 13 (or the minimum age

in your country). If you believe a child has sent us Personal Data, contact

us and we will delete it.
